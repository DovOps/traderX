### 2. Scope Document Review (20 minutes)
*Lead: Working Group Chair / FINOS Leadership*

**Discussion Points**:

#### 2.1 Strategic Rationale
- Does the rationale accurately capture member firm pain points?
- Are we positioning this correctly for board approval?

#### 2.2 Scope Boundaries
- Review "In Scope" and "Out of Scope" definitions
- Question: Are there critical areas missing or items that should be excluded?
- Confirm focus on OSS patching, maintainership, and EOL management

#### 2.3 Work Stream Structure
- **Work Stream 0**: Risk Measurement & Assessment
- **Work Stream 1**: Use Case Definition
- **Work Stream 2**: Prioritized Inventory & Overlap Analysis
- **Work Stream 3**: Target-State SLA Model

**Key Questions**:
- Are these work streams appropriately scoped?
- Do we have the right sequence and dependencies?
- Which work stream(s) should launch first?

#### 2.4 Success Criteria
- Review short-term, medium-term, and long-term goals
- Are these metrics measurable and achievable?

**Decision Required**: Approve scope document for board presentation (with amendments if needed)

### 3. Open Backpatch Alliance Opportunity (15 minutes)
*Lead: FINOS Leadership + [Moderne presenter if invited]*

#### 3.1 Strategic Fit
- How Backpatch Alliance aligns with initiative work streams
- Value proposition for FINOS members vs. commercial alternatives
- Moderne's proposed contribution (SLA-backed patches, automated distribution, tooling)

#### 3.2 Current Interest Level
- Organizations that have expressed interest: Citi, Fidelity, Capital One, BNY, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley
- Minimum threshold: 3 FINOS member firms to formalize working group

#### 3.3 Integration Approach
- Should Backpatch Alliance be the pilot implementation of this initiative?
- Timing: Launch in parallel with broader work streams, or sequence after scoping?
- Governance: How does it fit within FINOS structure?

**Key Questions**:
- Who is prepared to commit to Backpatch Alliance participation?
- What does "commitment" mean (resources, funding, governance participation)?
- Should we invite Moderne to present detailed proposal in next meeting?

**Decision Required**:
- Approve Backpatch Alliance as pilot project within initiative
- Identify 3+ firms willing to formally commit
- Decision on Moderne invitation for detailed presentation

### 4. Governance & Operating Model (10 minutes)
*Lead: FINOS Leadership*

#### 4.1 Working Group Structure
- Elect or nominate Working Group Chair

- Meeting cadence and decision-making process

#### 4.2 Member Commitments
- What level of participation is expected from member firms?

- Funding model (if applicable)

#### 4.3 Antitrust Compliance
- Framework for inventory sharing with legal safeguards
- Process for legal oversight and compliance review
- Documentation requirements

**Key Questions**:
